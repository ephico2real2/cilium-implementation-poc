## The two designs, and what was taken

Both designs agree on the structure: the instrumentations named in `requirements.txt` (never
`opentelemetry-bootstrap`), each `CMD` started by `opentelemetry-instrument`, traces only (metrics
and logs belong to R1.8 and R1.7), OTLP/HTTP to Envoy's `otlp.mesh`, W3C trace context, every
trace kept, `/metrics`, `/healthz` and `/readyz` not traced, and a phase 6 check that places one
order with its own `traceparent` and reads that exact trace from Tempo.
