## Second review pass: fixed head `ef69c5b`

The second pass refuted F1, F2 and F3 as implemented. Prometheus's `[310s]` rate at T is the
exact regular-scrape MetricsQL equivalent; its values at T−s and T+s are other windows and
admitted a dropped boundary sample, a store one scrape behind and a duplicated previous scrape.
The 32-sample gate could wait forever on a legitimately new or churning label set, while counts
alone did not prove spacing. The shared-gauge-hole exception queried VictoriaMetrics only at T,
so a copy that lost the series passed on Prometheus's neighbours alone.

The applied resolution compares rates only with Prometheus `[310s]` at T, checks both stores at
both gauge neighbours, and gates on the actual exact cross-engine comparisons after a complete
`up` window. Its 72 checks now end with an explicit timeout line. Phase 6 also requires positive,
not merely equal, MQ scrape counts. The second-pass findings and executable negative controls are
recorded with the review artefacts.
