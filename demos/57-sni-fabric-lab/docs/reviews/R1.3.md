## Round 1, measured on the live lab: three defects

| # | Found | Measurement | Decision |
|---|---|---|---|
| D1 | VictoriaMetrics scraping the same `prometheus.yml` took IBM MQ's gauges from Prometheus | Prometheus `scrape_samples_scraped{job="mq"}`: 136 every minute until 07:15 UTC, 108 from 07:16, when VictoriaMetrics' scraper started; the 28 missing names all `ibmmq_qmgr_*` publication gauges; the dashboard's "Queue manager: recovery log write latency" went empty on Prometheus. Two back-to-back reads of `/metrics/mq`: `first=1 second=0`. Cause, in `github.com/ibm-messaging/mq-container` `internal/metrics`: `exporter.go`'s `Collect()` resets each gauge vector and refills it from the publications cached since the last read; `update.go` then clears the cache | **One scraper.** Prometheus scrapes as before and remote-writes every sample to VictoriaMetrics through Envoy (`victoriametrics.mesh` on `net-obs`, `POST /api/v1/write`); VictoriaMetrics scrapes nothing. Measured with a temporary edit first: after 6 min both stores 136 MQ samples and the gauge, `vm_rows_inserted_total{type="promremotewrite"}` rising, `promscrape` 0. The R1.2 route test's refusal of `POST victoriametrics.mesh /api/v1/write` is inverted on purpose: any mesh caller could already write metrics through `POST /opentelemetry/v1/metrics`; the host-facing route stays query-only |
| D2 | `tests/parity.py`'s tolerance was the query's own movement over 10 s, about zero when traffic is steady | 7 of 10 disagreed on each of 12 tries. With identical samples (D1's design): exact agreement on component health, MQ put/got, MQ latency and the zero rates; Postgres within 0.1–0.3 %; low-count rates at 5/290 vs 5/300 (Prometheus extrapolates its 290 s of samples; VictoriaMetrics divides by the 300 s window, using the sample before it) or 4/290 vs 5/300 (one event at the window's edge) | **Tolerance from both engines' definitions**: VictoriaMetrics at T must lie within Prometheus's values at T − s, T and T + s, widened by m·s/W for the extrapolation. Parity runs once both stores hold a full window; a disagreement seen on two consecutive reads fails |
| D3 | The upgrade path left running containers on old bind-mounted files | After a `git switch` in the lab's checkout: `fabric-web-1` saw `/usr/share/nginx/html` empty (6 files on the host), Grafana's provisioning and dashboards empty, and `fabric-prometheus-1` read `prometheus.yml` md5 `43d4…` while the host's was `9c05…`.
