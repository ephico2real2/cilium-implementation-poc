## Found while applying: the R1.1 span check failed falsely after a restart

`compose up` recreated the collector and Prometheus at 00:41:07. The restarted collector has no
`otelcol_receiver_accepted_spans_total` series until its first span, and the new Prometheus
process never saw the old series, so it wrote no staleness marker: `sum(...)` kept returning the
old process's last value (86) for its 5-minute lookback. phase6 read `before=86, after=10` and
failed. **F5**: `prometheus_value()` reads `sum(last_over_time(...[20s]))`, the last two
scrapes (10 s apart). Proof: after restarting both, the old query returned a stale 20; phase6
then passed with `accepted spans 0 -> 10`.
